ABOUT ME!
======
I am a Business Informatics postgraduate student at the University of Debrecen, Hungary, with a background in Business and Information Technology.

I am interested in the intersection of business, technology and data-driven decision-making. My work focuses on exploring how information systems, data analytics and software solutions can address real-world organisational challenges.
